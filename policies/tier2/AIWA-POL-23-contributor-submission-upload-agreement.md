# Contributor Submission & Upload Agreement Policy

**Document ID:** AIWA-POL-23
**Version:** 0.1
**Status:** Draft
**Tier:** 2 — Operational & Platform Policies
**Scope:** Public-facing contributor rules governing submission authority, upload permissions, platform-use rights, cultural declarations, and contributor responsibility for uploaded materials.
**Restrictions:** This document states public contributor obligations and does not replace more specific rights-verification, licensing, and contractual controls applied in AIWA-POL-01, AIWA-POL-04, and AIWA-POL-05.
**Cross-Reference:** AIWA-DOC-02 (Legal Alignment), AIWA-POL-01 (Rights Administration & Licensing), AIWA-POL-04 (AI Governance & Dataset Stewardship), AIWA-POL-05 (Rights Verification & Provenance), AIWA-POL-06 (Content Standards), AIWA-POL-11 (Platform Enforcement & Appeals), AIWA-POL-17 (Platform Terms of Service)

---

## 1. Purpose

1.1. This document defines the rules that apply when a contributor uploads, submits, or registers material through AIWA services.

1.2. This document establishes contributor authority, warranties, permissions, cultural declarations, and responsibility for uploaded material.

1.3. Submission to AIWA is permitted only on the basis of truthful authority and documented rights responsibility.

---

## 2. Eligibility to Submit

2.1. A contributor may submit only material that the contributor created, owns, controls, or is expressly authorized to submit.

2.2. A contributor submitting on behalf of another person, estate, community, institution, school, or company must hold documented authority to do so.

2.3. AIWA may require identity documents, authority letters, agreements, or additional verification before activating or reviewing a submission.

2.4. Anonymous or pseudonymous submission for commercial administration is not permitted unless AIWA has verified the contributor's real identity and lawful basis internally.

---

## 3. Contributor Representations

3.1. By uploading material, a contributor represents that the submission is accurate and lawful.

3.2. The contributor represents that the contributor has disclosed all known co-owners, contributors, licences, restrictions, and prior assignments relevant to the submission.

3.3. The contributor represents that metadata, provenance statements, cultural declarations, and rights statements submitted to AIWA are materially complete and not misleading.

3.4. The contributor represents that submission of the material to AIWA does not knowingly violate any court order, law, embargo, confidentiality obligation, or binding third-party agreement.

---

## 4. Operational Rights Granted to AIWA

4.1. By uploading material, a contributor grants AIWA the limited non-exclusive rights necessary to receive, host, store, scan, classify, review, secure, transcode, analyse, moderate, and administer the submission within the AIWA system.

4.2. The rights granted under Section 4.1 include only the operational and administrative uses required for intake, rights verification, cultural review, preservation, compliance review, and any separately authorized exploitation or AI processing.

4.3. The upload itself does not grant AIWA a commercial exploitation right beyond what is expressly authorized in the applicable contributor agreement or licence instrument.

4.4. AIWA's use of SPARXSTAR as technical infrastructure does not transfer ownership of contributor content to AIWA, SPARXSTAR, or Starisian Technologies.

---

## 5. Third-Party Rights and Clearances

5.1. A contributor is responsible for obtaining all third-party permissions, licences, releases, and clearances required for the submitted material.

5.2. This responsibility includes samples, images, performances, voices, translations, adaptations, archive permissions, community permissions, and likeness or personality rights where applicable.

5.3. AIWA may request documentary proof of clearances before accepting, activating, licensing, or publishing the submission.

5.4. AIWA may Hold, Restrict, or Reject a submission where third-party rights are unclear, disputed, or unsupported.

---

## 6. Cultural and Community Declarations

6.1. A contributor must accurately state whether the submission includes folklore, sacred material, community-origin material, oral history, restricted language resources, or culturally sensitive content.

6.2. A contributor must not mislabel a modern work as folklore or mislabel folklore as a privately owned modern work.

6.3. Where community authority, elder consent, ministry input, NCAC input, or institutional clearance is required, the contributor must disclose that requirement and provide supporting evidence where available.

6.4. AIWA may require enhanced review and may restrict access, AI processing, or commercialization of culturally sensitive material.

---

## 7. AI-Related Permissions

7.1. Uploading material to AIWA does not by itself authorize AI processing, model training, dataset inclusion, or synthetic-output generation.

7.2. AI-related permissions require the separate consent and governance controls required by AIWA-POL-04.

7.3. A contributor must disclose any known material AI assistance used in creating the submitted work where that fact is relevant to rights, provenance, or platform labeling.

---

## 8. Prohibited Uploads

8.1. A contributor must not upload:

- material the contributor is not authorized to submit;
- infringing, deceptive, or fraudulent material;
- malware or code designed to disrupt the platform;
- restricted cultural material submitted in defiance of known authority restrictions;
- unlawful exploitative material involving minors; or
- synthetic or manipulated content intended to deceive as to origin, identity, or authority.

8.2. AIWA may immediately disable access to prohibited uploads where urgent action is required.

---

## 9. Removal, Withdrawal, and Records

9.1. A contributor may request withdrawal of a submission subject to applicable contractual commitments, licensing already granted, legal holds, audit needs, or public-interest obligations.

9.2. AIWA may retain records, evidence, and archived copies of a submission where required for rights verification, dispute resolution, security, fraud prevention, or legal compliance.

9.3. Removal of public access does not require deletion of all internal records where AIWA has a lawful retention basis.

---

## 10. Contributor Liability and Indemnity

10.1. A contributor remains responsible for the legality and authority of the contributor's submission.

10.2. Where AIWA reasonably relies on a contributor's false, incomplete, or misleading submission and suffers loss, claim, penalty, or enforcement consequence as a result, the contributor is responsible to the extent permitted by applicable law and the governing contributor agreement.

10.3. AIWA may hold payouts, suspend access, or seek recovery where contributor misrepresentation causes loss or verified third-party claims.

---

## 11. Enforcement and Appeals

11.1. Violations of this policy may result in a decision state of Held, Restricted, Rejected, or Escalated, together with suspension, removal, payout hold, or other enforcement under AIWA-POL-11.

11.2. A contributor may use the published review or appeal route where one applies to the relevant action.

11.3. AIWA may refer serious fraud, copyright abuse, or unlawful content to the competent public authority.

---

## 12. Relationship to Other Documents

12.1. This document supplements and does not replace the deeper rights-verification obligations in AIWA-POL-05.

12.2. Where a separate creator agreement, institutional submission agreement, or licensing agreement imposes stricter contributor obligations, the stricter obligation governs for that relationship.

12.3. In case of conflict, Gambian law and the hierarchy stated in AIWA-DOC-02 prevail.

---
