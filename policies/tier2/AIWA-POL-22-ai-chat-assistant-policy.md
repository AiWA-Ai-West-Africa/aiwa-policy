# AI Chat & Assistant Policy

**Document ID:** AIWA-POL-22
**Version:** 0.1
**Status:** Draft
**Tier:** 2 — Operational & Platform Policies
**Scope:** Public and operational rules governing AI chat, assistant, recommendation, and related conversational or generative AI features offered through AIWA services.
**Restrictions:** This document states AI interaction standards and does not replace dataset, licensing, privacy, or enforcement obligations in other AIWA policies.
**Cross-Reference:** AIWA-DOC-02 (Legal Alignment), AIWA-POL-04 (AI Governance & Dataset Stewardship), AIWA-POL-06 (Content Standards), AIWA-POL-08 (Educational & Informational Content), AIWA-POL-09 (Data Protection & Privacy), AIWA-POL-11 (Platform Enforcement), AIWA-POL-19 (Community Guidelines)

---

## 1. Purpose

1.1. This document defines the rules governing AI chat, assistant, and related generative AI features made available through AIWA services.

1.2. This document establishes transparency, safety, logging, prohibited-use, human-review, and output-governance requirements.

1.3. This document must be interpreted consistently with Gambian law, public-authority instructions, educational safeguards, and AIWA's creator-first and cultural-stewardship obligations.

---

## 2. Scope of AI Features

2.1. This policy applies to chatbots, writing assistants, educational assistants, recommendation systems with conversational outputs, translation assistants, moderation assistants, and similar AI-enabled interaction tools operated or made available by AIWA.

2.2. AIWA may use internal or third-party models for these features subject to AIWA's governance and vendor controls.

2.3. A feature described as AI-assisted, AI-generated, automated, or machine-generated is within the scope of this policy.

---

## 3. Transparency to Users

3.1. AIWA must identify AI chat and assistant features as AI-assisted or AI-generated in a clear manner.

3.2. AIWA must not present an AI system as a human staff member, legal adviser, teacher, elder, public official, or licensed professional.

3.3. Where an AI feature materially shapes a user-facing recommendation, moderation outcome, or educational response, AIWA must provide a route to human review where law or policy requires it.

---

## 4. Limitations and Reliance

4.1. AI outputs may be inaccurate, incomplete, biased, fabricated, stale, or contextually inappropriate.

4.2. AIWA must state that AI output is informational unless a separate human-reviewed service expressly states a higher assurance level.

4.3. Users must not rely on AI output as a substitute for legal advice, medical advice, mental-health advice, emergency guidance, professional accreditation, or governmental instruction.

4.4. AIWA may block or redirect prompts relating to high-risk domains including child safety, self-harm, regulated advice, election manipulation, or instructions for unlawful conduct.

---

## 5. User Responsibilities

5.1. Users must use AI features lawfully and responsibly.

5.2. Users must not use AI features to generate or distribute:

- hateful, abusive, or harassing material;
- unlawful sexual or exploitative material;
- deceptive political or civic manipulation;
- malware, credential theft, or fraud content;
- deepfakes or unauthorized voice or likeness replicas;
- infringing reproductions of protected works; or
- synthetic content presented as authentic cultural authority without disclosure.

5.3. Users remain responsible for their prompts, instructions, and downstream use of AI outputs.

---

## 6. Prompt, Input, and Output Handling

6.1. AIWA may log prompts, uploaded context, outputs, and usage metadata for safety review, abuse prevention, service improvement, incident response, and legal compliance.

6.2. AIWA must limit access to AI interaction logs to authorized personnel and authorized processors.

6.3. A user must not submit confidential third-party information, restricted cultural material, payment-card data, biometric data, or other sensitive information into AI chat features unless the feature expressly permits that use and the user has authority to provide it.

6.4. Retention and deletion of AI interaction data must follow AIWA-POL-09 and any product-specific notice.

---

## 7. Human Review and Escalation

7.1. AIWA must not rely solely on automated AI output for decisions that materially affect account access, payment rights, cultural restrictions, or legal position where human review is required by policy or law.

7.2. Moderation, rights, and safety flags generated by AI systems must be reviewable by an authorized human decision-maker before final material enforcement, except where immediate temporary action is required for urgent harm prevention.

7.3. Users must be able to request review of a material AI-assisted enforcement outcome through the route provided under AIWA-POL-11.

---

## 8. Cultural and Educational Safeguards

8.1. AI features must not override cultural restrictions, provenance conditions, or community controls applied elsewhere in the AIWA system.

8.2. AI features must not fabricate cultural authority, sacred meaning, or provenance claims.

8.3. Educational AI features must align with AIWA-POL-08 and must not present speculative output as verified educational fact.

8.4. Where a prompt concerns restricted cultural material, minors, public health, or public institutions, AIWA may limit response scope or require human handling.

---

## 9. Output Rights and Reuse

9.1. AIWA does not represent that all AI outputs are protectable intellectual property under applicable law.

9.2. A user receives only the rights in AI output that AIWA is lawfully able to grant under the governing service terms.

9.3. A user must not assume that AI output is free of third-party rights risk.

9.4. AIWA may require labeling, attribution, or use restrictions for AI outputs generated from or alongside AIWA-governed materials.

---

## 10. Prohibited System Uses

10.1. AIWA must not knowingly use AI chat or assistant features for unlawful surveillance, discriminatory profiling, deceptive public-authority impersonation, or cultural extraction contrary to AIWA policy.

10.2. AIWA must not authorize a feature configuration that materially increases the risk of deepfake abuse, biometric misuse, or unauthorized model training on governed content.

10.3. AIWA may suspend or withdraw an AI feature where safety, legal, vendor, or policy risk becomes unacceptable.

---

## 11. Incident Reporting and Enforcement

11.1. Suspected AI misuse, harmful outputs, or policy evasion must be reportable through AIWA's published support or enforcement channels.

11.2. AIWA may remove outputs, disable prompts, suspend access, preserve logs, or refer matters to public authorities where required.

11.3. Repeated or severe misuse of AI features may result in permanent feature restriction or account termination.

---

## 12. Updates

12.1. AIWA may update this policy as AI features, law, regulatory expectations, and risk controls evolve.

12.2. Material changes affecting logging, data use, user rights, or prohibited uses must be communicated through reasonable notice before taking effect where practicable.

---
