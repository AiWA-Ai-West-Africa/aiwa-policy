# Community Guidelines Policy

**Document ID:** AIWA-POL-19
**Version:** 0.1
**Status:** Draft
**Tier:** 2 — Operational & Platform Policies
**Scope:** Public-facing rules for respectful community participation, platform conduct, cultural respect, and safe interaction across AIWA services.
**Restrictions:** This document states behavioural standards. Formal enforcement and appeals remain governed by AIWA-POL-11.
**Cross-Reference:** AIWA-DOC-02 (Legal Alignment), AIWA-POL-03 (Cultural Heritage), AIWA-POL-06 (Content Standards), AIWA-POL-11 (Platform Enforcement), AIWA-POL-22 (AI Chat & Assistant)

---

## 1. Purpose

1.1. These guidelines define the expected standard of conduct for users interacting on AIWA services.

1.2. These guidelines support safe participation, cultural respect, educational integrity, and community trust.

1.3. These guidelines apply to posts, comments, messages, chat, submissions, marketplace interactions, profile text, usernames, events, and other user conduct on or through the platform.

---

## 2. Respectful Participation

2.1. Users must engage with others respectfully.

2.2. Users must not harass, bully, threaten, stalk, demean, or intimidate other persons.

2.3. Users must not target individuals or groups on the basis of ethnicity, tribe, nationality, language, religion, gender, disability, age, or other protected status.

2.4. Users must not organize abuse, pile-ons, retaliation campaigns, or coordinated intimidation.

---

## 3. Cultural Respect

3.1. Users must treat Gambian and West African cultural materials with respect.

3.2. Users must not mock, distort, misappropriate, or commercially exploit restricted or community-linked materials without authority.

3.3. Users must not falsely claim community authority, traditional status, or cultural endorsement.

3.4. Users must respect access controls, attribution requirements, and any stated restrictions attached to cultural material.

---

## 4. Truthfulness and Integrity

4.1. Users must not impersonate a creator, rights holder, moderator, elder, ministry, public authority, or AIWA representative.

4.2. Users must not submit deceptive metadata, falsified evidence, fabricated complaints, or manipulated provenance records.

4.3. Users must not spread materially false information about rights ownership, platform enforcement, payment status, or public-interest matters through the platform.

4.4. Users must clearly disclose AI-generated or heavily AI-assisted content where the context would otherwise mislead other users.

---

## 5. Safety and Illegal Harm

5.1. Users must not post, request, promote, or facilitate illegal content or activity.

5.2. Users must not publish content involving child sexual abuse material, sexual exploitation of minors, trafficking, terrorism support, credible threats, or direct incitement of violence.

5.3. Users must not use the platform to dox, blackmail, extort, or expose private personal information without lawful authority.

5.4. Users must not encourage self-harm, violent extremism, or criminal conduct.

---

## 6. Spam, Manipulation, and Platform Abuse

6.1. Users must not spam, mass-message, artificially amplify content, or manipulate rankings, metrics, reviews, or rewards.

6.2. Users must not use bots or automated scripts to evade rate limits, simulate engagement, or harvest data without authorization.

6.3. Users must not attempt to bypass platform safety, moderation, payment, or rights controls.

6.4. Users must not exploit bugs or security weaknesses for gain or disruption.

---

## 7. AI and Chat Conduct

7.1. Users must not use AI chat or assistant features to generate abuse, fraud, deepfakes, disinformation, unlawful advice, or culturally harmful content.

7.2. Users must not use AI features to impersonate living or deceased persons, cultural authorities, or public institutions.

7.3. Users must not prompt AI systems to reveal confidential data, evade safeguards, or generate infringing or restricted material.

7.4. AI interaction rules are further governed by AIWA-POL-22.

---

## 8. Marketplace and Commercial Conduct

8.1. Users must describe goods, rights, services, subscriptions, and offers truthfully.

8.2. Users must not engage in chargeback fraud, payment manipulation, false refund claims, sham transactions, or unauthorized resale.

8.3. Users must honour clearly stated delivery, cancellation, and redemption conditions unless applicable law requires a different outcome.

---

## 9. Reporting and Enforcement

9.1. Users are encouraged to report suspected violations in good faith.

9.2. AIWA may remove content, restrict interaction, suspend accounts, hold rewards, reverse transactions, or escalate matters to public authorities where required.

9.3. Repeated, severe, or coordinated violations may result in permanent removal from platform services.

9.4. Appeals and review rights are governed by AIWA-POL-11 where applicable.

---

## 10. Public-Interest and Youth Protections

10.1. Users must not use the platform to spread harmful misinformation relating to health, elections, public safety, or education.

10.2. Users must not target minors with exploitative, sexualized, manipulative, or age-inappropriate conduct.

10.3. Where a feature is used in school, youth, or public-interest contexts, AIWA may apply stricter moderation and access limits.

---

**End of AIWA-POL-19**
