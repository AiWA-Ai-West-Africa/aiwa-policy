# Cookie & Tracking Technologies Policy

**Document ID:** AIWA-POL-18
**Version:** 0.1
**Status:** Draft
**Tier:** 2 — Operational & Platform Policies
**Scope:** Public policy governing AIWA's use of cookies, similar technologies, consent controls, and related tracking disclosures.
**Restrictions:** This document defines disclosure and consent standards. It does not replace broader data-governance controls in AIWA-POL-09.
**Cross-Reference:** AIWA-POL-09 (Data Protection & Privacy), AIWA-POL-17 (Platform Terms of Service), AIWA-POL-20 (Terms of Sale & Subscriptions)

---

## 1. Purpose

1.1. This document defines how AIWA uses cookies and similar tracking technologies on its websites, applications, and platform interfaces.

1.2. This document establishes user notice, consent, management, and retention rules for tracking technologies.

1.3. This document must be interpreted consistently with Gambian data-protection law and any competent regulator guidance applicable to electronic communications and privacy.

---

## 2. Technologies Covered

2.1. This policy applies to cookies, local storage objects, pixels, SDK identifiers, session tokens, device identifiers, and similar technologies used to recognize a browser, session, device, or user.

2.2. A tracking technology is treated as a cookie under this policy where it performs a function materially similar to a cookie.

2.3. AIWA must maintain an internal register of material tracking technologies used on public-facing services.

---

## 3. Categories of Use

3.1. AIWA may use strictly necessary cookies for authentication, security, fraud prevention, load balancing, user-session continuity, and core service operation.

3.2. AIWA may use functional cookies to remember user settings, language preferences, accessibility preferences, and interface choices.

3.3. AIWA may use analytics cookies to measure service performance, traffic, error rates, feature use, and service improvement needs.

3.4. AIWA may use transaction-related cookies to manage shopping baskets, billing sessions, subscription status, entitlement validation, and payment security.

3.5. AIWA must not deploy advertising, profiling, or cross-site behavioural tracking technologies unless the lawful basis, disclosure, and user controls required by applicable law are in place.

---

## 4. Consent and Lawful Basis

4.1. Strictly necessary cookies may be used without opt-in consent where they are required to provide the requested service or maintain platform security.

4.2. Functional, analytics, advertising, or similar non-essential cookies must be deployed only on a valid lawful basis under applicable data-protection law.

4.3. Where consent is the required lawful basis, AIWA must obtain that consent before placing the relevant non-essential cookie.

4.4. Consent requests must be clear, granular, and separate from general acceptance of platform terms where law requires that separation.

4.5. A user's refusal of non-essential cookies must not block access to the core service except where the requested feature cannot technically operate without the relevant cookie.

---

## 5. User Controls

5.1. AIWA must provide a reasonably accessible method for users to manage cookie preferences.

5.2. A user must be able to withdraw consent for non-essential cookies as easily as consent was given.

5.3. AIWA must honour browser or device settings where technically feasible and where applicable law requires those settings to be respected.

5.4. AIWA must explain that disabling certain cookies may affect service performance, sign-in continuity, language settings, or purchase completion.

---

## 6. Third-Party Technologies

6.1. Where a third party places or reads cookies through the AIWA platform, AIWA must disclose the category and purpose of that third-party technology.

6.2. AIWA must not permit a third-party tracking technology on the platform unless there is a documented business purpose, contractual control, and lawful basis for its use.

6.3. Third-party payment, analytics, or embedded-media tools must be reviewed for privacy and security risk before deployment.

---

## 7. Retention and Security

7.1. Cookie retention periods must be proportionate to the purpose served.

7.2. Session cookies must expire when the session ends unless a longer period is technically necessary for security or transactional continuity.

7.3. Persistent cookies must have documented expiry periods and must not be retained longer than necessary for the stated purpose.

7.4. AIWA must protect cookie-related data against unauthorized access, misuse, and disclosure in line with AIWA-POL-09.

---

## 8. Updates and Contact

8.1. AIWA may update this policy when cookie use, legal requirements, or platform functionality changes.

8.2. Material changes affecting categories of tracking, consent requirements, or third-party access must be communicated through updated notice before taking effect where practicable.

8.3. Questions or requests relating to cookies and tracking technologies must be handled through AIWA's published privacy or support contact channel.
