# Rewards, Points & Credits Policy

**Document ID:** AIWA-POL-21
**Version:** 0.1
**Status:** Draft
**Tier:** 2 — Operational & Platform Policies
**Scope:** Rules governing promotional rewards, points, badges, service credits, internal-value mechanisms, and related anti-abuse controls.
**Restrictions:** This document governs internal programme value only. It does not create securities, stored-value, deposit, wage, or cash-equivalent rights.
**Cross-Reference:** AIWA-POL-17 (Platform Terms of Service), AIWA-POL-19 (Community Guidelines), AIWA-POL-20 (Terms of Sale, Subscriptions & Marketplace Transactions)

---

## 1. Purpose

1.1. This document defines the rules for AIWA rewards programmes, points systems, service credits, badges, and similar internal incentive mechanisms.

1.2. This document establishes earning, redemption, expiry, suspension, anti-fraud, and programme-change rules.

1.3. This document must be interpreted consistently with applicable Gambian consumer-protection, tax, anti-fraud, and promotional-law requirements.

---

## 2. Nature of Rewards

2.1. Points, rewards, badges, and service credits are contractual platform benefits only.

2.2. Unless AIWA expressly states otherwise in writing, rewards have no cash value, are not legal tender, are not redeemable for cash, and are not transferable property rights.

2.3. A reward does not create a bank account, trust entitlement, royalty right, wage entitlement, or equity interest.

2.4. AIWA may issue different classes of rewards for promotional, educational, community, or service-recovery purposes.

---

## 3. Eligibility

3.1. AIWA may set eligibility conditions for any rewards programme.

3.2. Eligibility may depend on age, territory, account standing, verified identity, subscription tier, contribution status, or lawful participation criteria.

3.3. AIWA may exclude users or territories where a rewards programme would create legal, tax, sanctions, gambling, or regulatory risk.

---

## 4. Earning Rules

4.1. Rewards may be granted for purchases, participation, educational activity, verified contributions, community support, promotions, or other stated activities.

4.2. A user acquires a reward only when AIWA records the reward to the user's account.

4.3. AIWA may reverse a pending or posted reward where the underlying transaction is refunded, reversed, fraudulent, abusive, or invalid.

4.4. AIWA may set earning caps, promotional windows, and category restrictions.

---

## 5. Redemption Rules

5.1. Rewards may be redeemed only for the uses expressly stated by AIWA.

5.2. Redemption may be subject to minimum thresholds, expiry dates, account verification, or feature availability.

5.3. AIWA may reject a redemption request where the account is suspended, under review, delinquent, or associated with abuse.

5.4. Once validly redeemed, a reward may be irreversible unless redemption failure, technical error, or legal obligation requires reversal.

---

## 6. Expiry and Forfeiture

6.1. AIWA may apply expiry dates to rewards, points, or credits.

6.2. Expiry terms must be disclosed at or before grant where practicable.

6.3. Rewards may be forfeited where the relevant programme ends, the account closes, the user breaches platform rules, or law requires cancellation.

6.4. AIWA may provide a grace period or notice before large-scale expiry where operationally reasonable, but this policy does not guarantee that notice in every case.

---

## 7. Non-Transferability

7.1. Rewards are personal to the eligible account unless AIWA expressly permits transfer.

7.2. Users must not sell, barter, gift, assign, pool, or broker rewards except through a platform function expressly created for that purpose.

7.3. AIWA may void rewards transferred or offered in violation of this policy.

---

## 8. Fraud and Abuse Controls

8.1. Users must not manipulate rewards programmes through fake accounts, sham transactions, bots, collusion, self-dealing, chargeback abuse, exploit use, or misleading activity.

8.2. AIWA may audit rewards activity and request supporting information.

8.3. AIWA may reverse rewards, suspend redemption, freeze an account, or terminate participation where abuse is suspected or verified.

8.4. Material fraud may be referred to payment processors, regulators, or law-enforcement authorities.

---

## 9. Promotional Campaigns

9.1. Specific campaigns, contests, or promotional draws may have additional rules.

9.2. Where campaign-specific rules conflict with this policy, the campaign-specific rules govern only for that campaign and only to the extent lawfully stated.

9.3. AIWA must not operate a promotion in a manner that would unlawfully constitute a lottery, gambling product, or misleading commercial practice.

---

## 10. Programme Changes and Termination

10.1. AIWA may modify, suspend, or terminate a rewards programme.

10.2. AIWA may change earning rates, redemption values, eligibility criteria, or expiry rules with prospective effect after reasonable notice where practicable.

10.3. Where a programme is terminated, AIWA may set a final redemption period, convert value to another internal benefit, or cancel unused rewards if law permits.

---

## 11. Complaints and Interpretation

11.1. Questions and complaints regarding rewards must be submitted through AIWA's published support channel.

11.2. AIWA may require account verification before resolving a rewards dispute.

11.3. This policy is interpreted consistently with AIWA's payment, enforcement, and community-conduct rules.

---
