# Terms of Sale, Subscriptions & Marketplace Transactions Policy

**Document ID:** AIWA-POL-20
**Version:** 0.1
**Status:** Draft
**Tier:** 2 — Operational & Platform Policies
**Scope:** Public policy governing digital sales, subscriptions, recurring billing, marketplace transactions, taxes, refunds, and payment disputes on AIWA services.
**Restrictions:** This document states transaction rules and does not replace separate negotiated commercial agreements for enterprise, institutional, or bespoke licensing deals.
**Cross-Reference:** AIWA-DOC-02 (Legal Alignment), AIWA-POL-09 (Data Protection & Privacy), AIWA-POL-17 (Platform Terms of Service), AIWA-POL-21 (Rewards, Points & Credits), AIWA-POL-23 (Contributor Submission & Upload Agreement)

---

## 1. Purpose

1.1. This document defines the rules that apply when AIWA sells digital goods, services, subscriptions, credits, licences, or marketplace offerings.

1.2. This document establishes billing, delivery, cancellation, refund, and payment-dispute standards.

1.3. This document must be interpreted consistently with Gambian contract, consumer-protection, tax, payments, and electronic-communications law, together with any stricter non-waivable law applicable to the transaction.

---

## 2. Covered Transactions

2.1. This policy applies to subscriptions, paid platform access, digital content sales, service credits, event access, archive access, licences, datasets, and other paid digital transactions offered through AIWA.

2.2. This policy also applies where AIWA facilitates a marketplace or mediated transaction between users, except to the extent a separate marketplace-specific agreement states additional terms.

2.3. Institutional, publishing, or enterprise rights deals documented in bespoke contracts may override this policy only to the extent expressly stated in writing.

---

## 3. Pricing and Order Formation

3.1. AIWA must present the price, currency, and material transaction terms before checkout or binding order placement.

3.2. Taxes, levies, processing fees, or third-party charges must be disclosed before completion of the transaction where they are known at checkout.

3.3. An order is accepted only when AIWA or the relevant seller confirms the order or activates access.

3.4. AIWA may refuse or cancel an order before fulfilment where there is pricing error, fraud risk, sanctions risk, legal restriction, inventory or entitlement error, or unresolved rights uncertainty.

---

## 4. Payments

4.1. Users must provide a valid payment method authorized for the transaction.

4.2. AIWA may use third-party payment processors to collect and settle payments.

4.3. AIWA may perform fraud screening, identity checks, transaction monitoring, and billing verification before or after payment authorization.

4.4. A user is responsible for bank, card-network, mobile-money, foreign-exchange, or other external charges imposed by the user's provider unless law provides otherwise.

---

## 5. Digital Delivery and Access

5.1. Digital delivery occurs when access, download, entitlement, activation, or account functionality is made available to the purchaser.

5.2. Delivery times are estimates unless AIWA expressly states a guaranteed delivery timeline.

5.3. AIWA may delay delivery where rights verification, cultural review, payment confirmation, export control, sanctions review, or public-authority clearance is required.

5.4. Access to purchased digital services may be limited by territory, account status, content classification, or technical compatibility.

---

## 6. Subscriptions and Auto-Renewal

6.1. A recurring subscription renews automatically only where the auto-renewal terms are clearly disclosed before purchase.

6.2. Subscription terms must state the billing interval, renewal timing, cancellation path, and the point at which changes take effect.

6.3. A user may cancel a subscription through the method made available by AIWA before the next billing cycle begins.

6.4. Cancellation stops future renewal charges but does not automatically reverse charges already validly incurred for the active term unless law requires otherwise.

6.5. AIWA may suspend or terminate subscription access for failed payment, chargeback abuse, sanctions risk, or material breach of platform rules.

---

## 7. Refunds and Cancellations

7.1. Unless non-waivable law provides otherwise, digital goods, activated subscriptions, fulfilled access rights, and consumed service credits are generally non-refundable after delivery or activation.

7.2. AIWA may issue a refund, replacement, service extension, or account credit where:

- AIWA charged the user in error;
- the service was not delivered as described in a material respect;
- duplicate payment occurred;
- fraudulent use of a payment method is verified; or
- applicable law requires a refund.

7.3. Refund requests must be reviewed on documented evidence and within published support timeframes.

7.4. Where a refund is denied, AIWA must communicate the basis for denial.

---

## 8. Chargebacks and Payment Disputes

8.1. A user must first attempt to resolve a billing issue through AIWA support where practicable before initiating an external chargeback.

8.2. AIWA may contest chargebacks that are fraudulent, duplicative, abusive, or unsupported by the transaction record.

8.3. Where a chargeback is initiated, AIWA may temporarily suspend the relevant account, subscription, entitlement, or payout pending resolution.

8.4. Repeated payment abuse may result in permanent transaction restrictions.

---

## 9. Marketplace Role Allocation

9.1. Where AIWA facilitates a transaction between a buyer and a third-party seller, AIWA must state whether AIWA acts as principal seller, disclosed agent, payment facilitator, or platform intermediary.

9.2. A marketplace seller remains responsible for the legality, accuracy, and rights status of the seller's listing unless AIWA expressly assumes that responsibility in writing.

9.3. AIWA may remove listings, hold settlement, or cancel transactions where rights, safety, fraud, cultural, or regulatory risk is identified.

---

## 10. Taxes, Records, and Compliance

10.1. Users are responsible for taxes arising from their purchases except to the extent AIWA is legally required to collect and remit them.

10.2. AIWA must maintain transaction records for accounting, audit, consumer-protection, fraud, and legal-compliance purposes.

10.3. AIWA may request additional information to comply with anti-money-laundering, know-your-customer, sanctions, export-control, or tax obligations.

---

## 11. Policy Changes

11.1. AIWA may amend this policy by publishing an updated version with an effective date.

11.2. Material changes affecting recurring billing, refund eligibility, cancellation rights, or user financial obligations must be communicated with reasonable advance notice unless immediate change is required by law, payment-network rules, or security necessity.
