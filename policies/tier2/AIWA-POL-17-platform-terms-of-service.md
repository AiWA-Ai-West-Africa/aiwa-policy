## 1. Purpose

1.1. This document defines the core terms that apply to use of the AIWA platform and related services.

1.2. This document establishes account, submission, payment, AI interaction, enforcement, and dispute rules for platform users.

1.3. This document operates as the master public-facing platform agreement unless a separate written agreement expressly overrides a specific term.

1.4. This document must be interpreted consistently with Gambian law, AIWA-DOC-02, and any binding instruction of a competent Gambian court, ministry, regulator, or public authority.

## 2. Scope and Acceptance

2.1. These terms apply to every person or entity that accesses, browses, submits to, purchases from, or uses the AIWA platform.

2.2. Use of the platform constitutes acceptance of these terms and of the policies incorporated by reference.

2.3. If a user does not accept these terms, that user must not use the platform.

2.4. Where a user acts on behalf of an organization, that user represents that they have authority to bind that organization.

2.5. These terms do not transfer ownership of user content to AIWA, SPARXSTAR, or Starisian Technologies.

## 3. Eligibility and Accounts

3.1. A user must provide accurate, current, and complete registration information.

3.2. A user must keep account credentials confidential and must not share access except as expressly permitted for an authorized organization account.

3.3. A user is responsible for activity occurring through the user's account unless the user has promptly reported unauthorized access.

3.4. A user must be legally capable of entering a binding agreement under applicable law.

3.5. A minor may use the platform only with the authorization and supervision of a parent, guardian, school, or other legally authorized adult or institution where applicable law permits.

3.6. AIWA may refuse registration, require identity verification, or limit account functionality where needed for compliance, fraud prevention, child protection, sanctions screening, or rights verification.

4.1. AIWA may provide rights administration, content intake, cultural review, archive access, educational services, AI tools, marketplace functions, subscriptions, and related digital services.

4.2. Platform functionality may vary by territory, account type, content classification, and applicable law.

4.3. AIWA may add, suspend, withdraw, or modify platform features where necessary for security, compliance, operational integrity, cultural protection, or lawful business reasons.

4.4. Platform availability is not guaranteed at all times.

4.5. Access to specific services may depend on completion of identity, rights, payment, or cultural-review requirements.

## 5. User Submissions and Platform Rights

5.1. A user must submit only content, metadata, and materials the user is authorized to submit.

5.2. A contributor's upload obligations, warranties, and authority requirements are governed by AIWA-POL-23 and AIWA-POL-05.

5.3. By submitting content, a user grants AIWA the limited operational rights necessary to host, store, review, format, secure, display, process, moderate, and administer that content within the AIWA system and any authorized channels.

5.4. The rights in Section 5.3 are non-exclusive, limited to the authorized purpose, revocable in accordance with the applicable agreement, and do not transfer ownership.

5.5. A user must not submit malware, unlawful content, deceptive metadata, infringing content, restricted cultural material without authority, or prohibited AI-generated material.

6.1. AI chat, assistant, recommendation, moderation, search, tagging, or translation features are governed by AIWA-POL-22 in addition to these terms.

6.2. AI-generated outputs may be inaccurate, incomplete, biased, or unsuitable for a specific use.

6.3. A user must not rely on AI output as legal, medical, financial, educational accreditation, or regulatory advice without independent human review.

6.4. AIWA may log prompts, outputs, and interaction data for safety, auditing, abuse prevention, quality control, and lawful compliance.

6.5. AIWA may restrict, review, or remove AI interactions that create legal, cultural, safety, misinformation, or rights risk.

7.1. Paid services, subscriptions, marketplace transactions, and other sales are governed by AIWA-POL-20 in addition to these terms.

7.2. A user must pay all fees, taxes, and charges associated with the user's purchases unless applicable law requires otherwise.

## 8. Acceptable Use and Community Conduct

8.1. A user must comply with AIWA-POL-06 and AIWA-POL-19 at all times.

8.2. A user must not:

- violate applicable law;
- infringe intellectual property or personality rights;
- harass, abuse, threaten, or impersonate any person;
- misuse AI features or attempt prompt-based policy evasion;
- scrape, exfiltrate, reverse engineer, or overload the platform without authorization;
- interfere with platform security, moderation, or audit controls; or
- use the platform in a way that undermines Gambian cultural protections, child protection, or public-interest obligations.

8.3. AIWA may investigate suspected misuse and preserve evidence for enforcement, regulatory response, or legal process.
